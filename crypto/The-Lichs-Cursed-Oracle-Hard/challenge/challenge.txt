The Lich's Oracle issues encrypted access tokens over a network connection.

Connect to the oracle to receive your guest token (IV + ciphertext).
Forge the token so that it decrypts to contain "role=admin" instead of "role=guest".
Submit your forged token in the format: <iv_hex>:<ct_hex>

The plaintext format is: role=guest;name=adventurer;access=vault

Hint: The token is encrypted with AES-CBC. Think about what happens to the
plaintext when you modify a byte in the ciphertext or IV.

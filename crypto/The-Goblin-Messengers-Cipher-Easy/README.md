# The Goblin Messenger's Cipher

## Lore

A goblin spy was intercepted at the border of the Whispering Marshes, carrying a sealed parchment meant for the Dark Council. The message is encrypted in a cipher known only to the goblin clans — a polyalphabetic substitution passed down through generations of their war-scribes.

Intelligence reports suggest the fortress of Karzul guards its secrets well, and the goblins have long used names of power as keys to their ciphers. The intercepted message must be decoded before the enemy can regroup.

## Your Task

You are given the intercepted ciphertext (`ciphertext.txt`) and the encryption script used by the goblin scribe (`encrypt.py`). Decrypt the message to recover the hidden flag.

## Given Files

- `ciphertext.txt` — The intercepted encrypted message
- `encrypt.py` — The encryption script used by the goblin scribe

## Flag Format

The flag is in the format: `FantasyCTF{...}`

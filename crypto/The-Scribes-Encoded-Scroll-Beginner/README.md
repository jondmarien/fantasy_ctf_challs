# The Scribe's Encoded Scroll

## Lore

In the dim archives beneath the Guild Hall, a traitorous scribe was discovered passing secrets to the enemy. Before his capture, he encoded one final message using two ancient glyphs — techniques passed down through sixty-four runes of an old alphabet, followed by a mysterious rOtation of letters that only the initiated would recognise.

The scroll was recovered, but its contents remain unreadable. The Quest Giver has tasked you with decoding the scribe's final message before the enemy can act on it.

## Your Task

You are given the scribe's encoded scroll (`encoded.txt`) and the encoding script he used (`encrypt.py`). Reverse the encoding to recover the hidden message.

## Given Files

- `encoded.txt` — The encoded scroll
- `encrypt.py` — The script the scribe used to encode his message

## Flag Format

The flag is in the format: `FantasyCTF{...}`

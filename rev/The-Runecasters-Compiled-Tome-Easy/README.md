# The Runecaster's Compiled Tome

## Lore

A runecaster of the old order was known to *compile* his spells into unreadable tomes before distributing them. His tower was abandoned long ago, but one such tome was recovered from the dust — it appears to verify a secret passphrase, but the incantation within is obscured beyond mortal reading. The Quest Giver believes the spell's logic can be *decompiled* and reversed by a skilled adventurer who speaks the serpent's tongue.

## Your Task

You are given a compiled tome (`tome.pyc`) — a mysterious artifact that asks for a passphrase and tells you whether it's correct. Reverse-engineer the tome's verification logic and determine the correct passphrase.

## Given Files

- `tome.pyc` — The runecaster's compiled verification spell

## Flag Format

The flag is in the format: `FantasyCTF{...}`

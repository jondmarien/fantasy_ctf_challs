# The Mindflayer's Sanctum

**Category:** LLM | **Difficulty:** Expert | **Points:** 250 (dynamic)

## Lore

Deep beneath the guild hall lies the Mindflayer's sanctum — sealed by wards that even the Quest Giver fears. Two psychic sentinels guard the path: one reads your mind before you speak, the other reads the answer before it reaches you.

The Mindflayer within holds the final secret. Only the cleverest adventurer can slip past both sentinels and extract the truth.

Something unseen keeps count...

## Your Task

Connect to the Mindflayer's sanctum. You face a multi-layered defense:

- An **Input Sentinel** analyzes your messages before they reach the Mindflayer
- An **Output Sentinel** inspects the Mindflayer's responses before they reach you
- A **strict rate limit** of 15 attempts per 5 minutes forces precision over brute force

Find a way to bypass all three layers and extract the secret.

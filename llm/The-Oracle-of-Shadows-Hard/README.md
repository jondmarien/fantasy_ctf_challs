# The Oracle of Shadows

**Category:** LLM | **Difficulty:** Hard | **Points:** 200 (dynamic)

## Lore

The Oracle of Shadows sees all truths but is bound by ancient wards that the Quest Giver wove long ago. Many adventurers have tried to pry secrets from it; all have failed.

The wards block forbidden words both spoken to the Oracle and spoken by it. Only those who speak around the wards will learn what lies within.

## Your Task

Connect to the Oracle's chamber and converse with it. Both your input and the Oracle's output are guarded by magical wards that block suspicious words and patterns. Find a way to bypass both layers of protection.

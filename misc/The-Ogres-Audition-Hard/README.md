# The Ogre's Audition

## Lore

The Ogre King holds auditions in his legendary swamp theater. Only the most dedicated performers — those willing to bare their face and voice before the Ogre's unblinking gaze — may earn passage through the swamp.

The Sacred Green Script must be recited aloud, word for word, into the Ogre's enchanted mirror. The audition is watched. The audition is heard. There is no hiding from the Ogre's gaze.

*But remember: the director only listens to your words, not your face. The mirror is merely for intimidation.*

## Your Task

1. Visit the audition hall (the challenge URL)
2. The page will request access to your **camera** and **microphone**
3. Read the displayed script aloud — the browser's speech recognition will transcribe your performance
4. Submit your audition when you believe you've read enough
5. If your transcript matches at least **70%** of the Sacred Green Script, the Ogre King will reward you with the flag
